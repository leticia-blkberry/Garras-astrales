Get involved

It takes a galaxy

Garras Astrales is run entirely by volunteers. Here's how you can join the collective.

Foster a cat

Fostering is the heart of our rescue. You provide a safe space and love; we provide food, litter, supplies, and all veterinary care.

We especially need fosters for:

  • Bottle babies and young kittens
  • Mamas with litters
  • Cats recovering from medical care
  • Shy cats who need time to trust
Apply to foster

What fosters get

  • All supplies and vet care covered
  • A mentor and a supportive group chat
  • Flexible commitment — weeks to months
  • The joy of seeing your foster cat find a home
