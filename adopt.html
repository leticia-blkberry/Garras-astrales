Find your feline constellation

Each of our cats lives with a foster family, so we know their personalities well. Tell us who caught your eye and we'll help you find the right match.

How adoption works

  1. Browse & reach out. Tell us which cat you're interested in, or describe what you're looking for.
  2. Application. A short form about your home and experience with cats.
  3. Meet & greet. Visit the cat at their foster home or a video call.
  4. Welcome home. Sign the adoption agreement, pay the fee, and start your new life together.
