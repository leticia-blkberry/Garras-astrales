A collective devoted to rescuing and caring for cats

Every cat deserves a place among the stars.

We rescue cats in need, care for them in loving foster homes, and match them with families who'll love them for life. Ready to adopt? Meet our cats. Not ready yet? There are plenty of other ways to help.

Meet the cats looking for a home

Every cat lives with a foster family, so we know their personalities well and can help you find the right match.

Adopting is simple

  1. Say hello. Tell us which cat caught your eye.
  2. Meet them. Visit at their foster home or by video.
  3. Welcome them home. We'll help you settle in.

Can't adopt right now? You can still help

Not every home is ready for a cat, and that's okay. Every one of these makes a real difference.

Foster

Open your home to a cat for a few weeks or months. We cover food, supplies, and vet care, and you're never on your own.

Become a foster →

Volunteer

Drive cats to the vet, help at adoption events, take photos, or lend a hand with admin. Give whatever time you have.

Find a way to volunteer →

Donate

$25 vaccinates a kitten. $75 covers a spay or neuter surgery. Every gift goes straight to the cats.

Make a gift →
