About us

Garras Astrales Collective

"Garras Astrales" means "Astral Claws" — a nod to the little night-sky wanderers we've dedicated ourselves to.

Our mission

We are a cat-only rescue collective. We rescue cats from the streets and from unsafe situations, provide medical care and socialization through our foster network, and match them with loving, permanent homes.

We also run trap-neuter-return (TNR) work to humanely reduce the number of cats born without a home.
